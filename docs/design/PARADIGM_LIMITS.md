# PARADIGM LIMITS — 11 problems of a real product (2026-08-30)

Source: an analysis of "what if dnaContract goes public." Format of each:
problem → move WITHIN the paradigm → what the move does NOT fix → integration into v1.
The through-line (and also the boundary of the promise): the paradigm converts
uncertainty from SILENT into VISIBLE, MEASURABLE and REVOCABLE.
No more than that — and that is already a lot.

## A. Limits of the guarantee
1. GUARANTEE ∝ 1/REALISM. A real product is mostly islands;
   the court covers the core. Move: we prove containment (membrane+drift+REVOKE,
   have: D43), not content. Does not fix: the island's content is unknown.
   INTEGRATION: U10 GUARANTEE PASSPORT — a first-class compiler output:
   "proved / assumed / monitored / weakest seam." A thin core =
   a measurable interface, not hidden shame.
2. THE MIDDLE OF JUDGMENT (taste, sensible retries, murky regulation). Move:
   a third fabric — skills, gates-as-properties = specification (have: D27).
   Does not fix: an unformalizable property -> island with manual accept.
   INTEGRATION: manual accept = a SIGNED assumption in the ledger (ties to §9).
3. WRONG-BUT-PASSES. Skill gates are weaker than the court. Move: adversarial
   hardening — every escape = a missing mutant/property/probe.
   Does not fix: the gates' race against the model's error space is not guaranteed.
   INTEGRATION: U11 HARDENING — an escape as a first-class ledger record +
   `onto harden <escape>`: incident -> regression|mutant|probe. Losing
   the race is always VISIBLE — a different regime than losing silently.

## B. Knowledge and the world
4. INTERVIEW WITHOUT AN ORACLE: nobody knows the answer. Move: islands applied
   to KNOWLEDGE — not-knowing = a typed hole: assumption-Expr + monitor +
   quota; the organism lives under the declared assumption. Does not fix: the world
   is not obligated to answer. INTEGRATION: U12 NOT-KNOWING — a third legal outcome
   of the interview, "I don't know" (patch A | patch B | assumption+monitor).
5. A MESH OF OTHERS' TRUTHS: system guarantee = min over the weakest membrane.
   Move: we manage the seams (have: a membrane on every seam). Does not fix: there are
   more seams than core. INTEGRATION: the passport (U10) aggregates all seams and
   NAMES the weakest one by name. Since it's a min — let the min be visible.

## C. Evolution and operation
6. FUNCTOR vs IRREVERSIBLE MESS. Move: the functor is for your own (have: D-migrations);
   others'/irreversible = island with rollback; lossiness — via interview-ack
   (have: ack_behavior_change). Does not fix: a loss remains a loss.
   INTEGRATION: migration type lossy with declared_loss in the ledger — signed,
   visible, legalized.
7. ON-CALL AT 3 AM. Move: a failure -> ledger slice + counterexample; explain
   O(k) (have: explain, /ops D69). Does not fix: not tested by a real
   incident. INTEGRATION: an incident runbook in OPERATOR.md; the exam itself —
   F7/calendar (unpassable from within, already in the remainder).
8. THE MODEL = SPOF OF THE GROWTH EDGE. Move: behind the frontier — model-independence
   via cache (have: D6). Does not fix: the edge stalls without a model. INTEGRATION:
   exam fcold — a COLD REBUILD with the key physically pulled:
   a checkable guarantee, not a declarative one.

## D. Trust and delivery
9. INSTITUTIONS TRUST CODE. Move: a reviewable unit = (genome diff +
   court certificate + signed assumptions + acks). Does not fix: the institution
   is not obligated to accept; "who's to blame" is an org question. INTEGRATION: U10
   `onto attest` — one signable release artifact: genome hash, court verdicts,
   assumptions, skill certificates, engine version.
10. STACK GATES ARE HANDMADE. Move: a fabric = an amortizable package
    (gates+emitter+conformance); the emitter grows with the model (have: D68).
    Does not fix: the first organism on a stack pays full price. INTEGRATION:
    "fabric as a publishable package" — second echelon (gene pool/marketplace).
11. THE ENGINE IS FOREVER IN THE TRUST BASE. Move: the engine = a proof-checker: small,
    frozen (have: D72), versioned. Does not fix: the engine's evolution and supply
    chain — a risk for every organism. INTEGRATION: pin the engine version
    into the organism-project (onto new) + the rule "an engine upgrade = a molt with
    a full gate run, not a silent merge."
